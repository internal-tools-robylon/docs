## View Incoming Tickets

The **main screen** displays all tickets received through your support email. Each ticket shows:

- **Ticket ID**
- **Subject line**
- **Status** (open, pending, closed)
- **Assigned agent** (if applicable)

## Filter Your View

Use the **filter buttons** to quickly narrow down tickets.\
Examples include:

- **Open tickets** only
- **Closed tickets** for QA and review
- **Pending tickets** waiting on customer response

## Inspect Ticket Details

Click on a ticket to open the **detailed view**, which includes:

- **Full conversation history** — all customer and agent exchanges
- **Issue classification** — along with the AI’s reasoning behind the label
- **Internal Notes** — visible only to your team within the Robylon platform
- **External Notes** — synced to your integrated ticketing platform (e.g., Zendesk), not visible to the customer
- **Context view** — ticket properties, customer information, and a chronological timeline of events

<Note>
  Use **Internal Notes** to log debug information or escalation steps. Use **External Notes** for coordination inside Zendesk or Freshdesk without alerting the customer.
</Note>
