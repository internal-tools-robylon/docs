## View All Chats

In the **main chat list**, you can see every conversation with:

- **Chat ID**
- **Resolution time**
- **Current agent** (if assigned)
- **Status** — **open**, **closed**, or **bot-handled**

## Inspect a Conversation

Click any chat to open its **conversation view**, which includes:

- **Full conversation history**
- **User details** (profile, contact information)
- **Captured variables** used during the interaction

## Take Control of a Chat

From the detailed view, a **human agent** can take over from the bot at any point—useful when:

- A customer **asks for escalation**
- The issue **requires human judgment**
- The bot triggers an **internal handoff rule**

<Note>
  Taking control **pauses the bot** and **assigns the conversation** to you.
</Note>

## Use Debug Mode for Troubleshooting

Enable **Debug Mode** to reveal:

- **Which AI agents** were triggered
- **What tools** were called
- The **decision path** taken by the workflow

This is valuable for refining workflows, diagnosing unexpected behavior, and auditing automation.

## Filter Chats

Use the **filters** at the top of the tab to find specific conversations by:

- **Agent**
- **Status** — open, closed, pending
- **Date range**

## View Human-Handled Chats

Open the **Human** tab to see chats **closed by agents**. This helps managers and QA review:

- **Escalation frequency**
- **Agent resolution quality**
- **Trends** in conversations requiring manual handling

## View & Control Queued Chats

The **Queue** tab shows chats waiting to be **assigned or handed over** to a human. Supervisors can track:

- **Number of chats** currently waiting
- **Average wait time** before pickup
- **Peak load periods** when queue size grows

## Details Pane

The **Details** pane (right side) surfaces structured context for faster, safer resolutions.

### User Info

- **User** identifier (e.g., phone or handle)
- **Phone / Email** — masked by default; click **Show** to reveal
- **View More** — expand to see additional profile attributes

<Tip>
  Keep **PII masked** by default. Reveal only when necessary to comply with privacy policies.
</Tip>

### Issue Info

- Current **issue classification** (if available)
- Links or references to **related tickets/cases**

### Tags

- Add or remove **tags** to aid routing, reporting, and QA.
- Use a consistent **taxonomy** (e.g., `billing`, `return`, `priority-high`).

### Variables

- All conversation **variables** captured or set by workflows (e.g., `order_id`, `is_vip`, `intent`).
- Variables power **conditions**, **tools**, and **personalized replies**.

<Note>
  If you don’t see expected variables, enable **Debug Mode** to verify captures and tool outputs.
</Note>

### Recent Conversations

- Quick access to the user’s **recent chats**, enabling faster context gathering and trend spotting.

### Actions

- **Share** conversation context (according to your org’s permissions).
- Copy **Chat ID** or key fields for escalations and handoffs.

---

## Best Practices

- Keep **Debug Mode** on during testing; turn it off for routine operations.
- Add **tags** consistently for better analytics and SLA reporting.
- Verify **variables** before calling tools that depend on them.
- Use the **Queue** tab to balance agent load during peaks.
