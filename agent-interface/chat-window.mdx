

# Guide: How to Manage Conversations in the Chat Window

The **Chat tab** is the central hub for all live customer chats. From here, you can view active and resolved chats, inspect details, and take actions as an agent.

---

## View All Chats

In the **main chat view**, you can see a list of all customer conversations with details such as:

- **Chat ID**
- **Resolution time**
- **Current agent** (if assigned)
- **Status** (open, closed, or bot-handled)

---

## Inspect a Conversation

Click on a chat to open its detailed view. This panel shows:

- The **full conversation history**
- **User details** (profile, contact information)
- All associated **variables** captured during the chat

---

## Take Control of a Chat

From the detailed chat view, a **human agent** can take over from the bot at any point.\
This is useful when:

- A customer asks for escalation
- The issue requires human judgment
- The bot triggers an internal handoff rule

<note>
  Taking control will pause the bot and assign the conversation to you.
</note>

---

## Use Debug Mode for Troubleshooting

Enable **Debug Mode** in the conversation view to see:

- Which **AI agents** were triggered
- What **tools** were called
- The decision path taken by the workflow

This is valuable for refining workflows, diagnosing unexpected behavior, and auditing automation.

## Filter Chats

Use the **filter options** at the top of the chat tab to find specific conversations. You can filter by:

- **Agent**
- **Status** (open, closed, pending)
- **Date range**

---

## View Human-Handled Chats

Click on the **Human tab** to see a filtered list of chats closed by human agents.\
This helps managers and QA teams review:

- Escalation frequency
- Agent resolution quality
- Trends in conversations requiring manual handling
