This is a guide to create a seamless customer journey by deploying your Robylon AI agent under your own domain. With custom domains, all Robylon branding is removed, so your chatbot appears as an integral part of your business, no third-party labels, just your brand front and center.

To get started, simply enter your preferred URL for the AI agent. Next, update your DNS settings with your domain provider (such as Cloudflare, OpenDNS, or Quad9). 

You’ll need to add a CNAME record using the details provided on your Robylon dashboard’s custom domain page. 

## What Does “Custom Domain” Mean on Robylon?

When you enable a custom domain on Robylon, your AI agent is hosted on a subdomain that belongs to your business, rather than using Robylon’s default domain. For example, instead of your AI agent appearing at robylon.ai/yourbot, it can be accessed at support.yourcompany.com or any subdomain you choose.

This feature brings your AI-powered support fully under your brand’s umbrella, enhancing both professionalism and customer trust. With a custom subdomain:

- Your AI agent feels like a natural extension of your website.
- Visitors interact directly with your domain, with no redirects to external Robylon URLs.
- The user experience is seamless, with consistent branding across your website and AI-powered support.
