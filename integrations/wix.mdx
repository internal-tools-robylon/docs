**Step 1: Generate and Copy Your Robylon Chatbot Embed Code.**

1. Log in to your Robylon account and open the dashboard.
2. In the left panel, click on the settings button to navigate to the settings page.

   <Frame>
     ![Robylon1 Pn](/Robylon1.png)
   </Frame>
3. Within the settings tab, select [<u>Channels</u>](https://app.robylon.ai/settings/channels).

   <Frame>
     ![Robylon2 Pn](/Robylon2.png)
   </Frame>
4. Click on the [<u>Chatbot</u>](https://app.robylon.ai/settings/channels/chatbot/interface) widget to access the chatbot details.

   <Frame>
     ![Robylon3 Pn](/Robylon3.png)
   </Frame>
5. Navigate to the [<u>Install</u>](https://app.robylon.ai/settings/channels/chatbot/install) tab within the chatbot details page.

   <Frame>
     ![Robylon4 Pn](/Robylon4.png)
   </Frame>
6. Select React to embed code in Wix.

   <Frame>
     ![Robylon5js Pn](/Robylon5React.png)
   </Frame>
7. Copy the provided chatbot embed code.

   <Frame>
     ![Robylon6js Pn](/images/Robylon6React.png)
   </Frame>

**Step 2**: Sign in to Your Wix Account and Embed Your Chatbot

1. Login to your Wix account and go to the dashboard.
2. On your dashboard, locate and click on the ‘Design Site’ button in the top right corner of the dashboard.

   <Frame>
     ![Wix1 Pn](/images/Wix1.png)
   </Frame>
3. Select the site you want to add the Robylon chatbot to. Click and load that site into your Wix editor.
4. Scroll down to any section of the page you want to add the Robylon chatbot.
5. Click on the Big Plus icon (Add Elements) on the left sidebar of your Wix editor.

   <Frame>
     ![Wix2 Pn](/images/Wix2.png)
   </Frame>
6. Scroll down to locate and click on the ‘Embed Code’ button, then click on the ‘Marketing Tools’ button and select ‘Custom Code’ in that section.

   <Frame>
     ![Wix3 Pn](/images/Wix3.png)
   </Frame>
7. The custom code widget should pop up, click ‘\+ Add Custom Code’ in the top right corner of that widget.

   <Frame>
     ![Wix4 Pn](/images/Wix4.png)
   </Frame>


11. Paste the copied code snippet into the custom code editor and provide a name for your code.
12. Choose an option under ‘Add Code to Pages’.
13. Click where you want to place your chatbot under the  ‘Place Code’ section.
14. Click Apply and preview your website, and there you should see the floating Robylon chat icon on your website.
